Two stages, two approaches

Guidance that matches your next decision.

Class 10 is about discovering suitable directions. Class 12 is about refining a chosen stream or career into a course, specialisation and college shortlist.

Class 10 · Explore
🧭

Discover what could fit you

Explore interests, reasoning, quantitative aptitude, problem-solving and work preferences. Get stream and subject-combination ideas, career families, college routes and next steps.

🎯

Turn your direction into a plan

Choose your current stream and intended field. Compare relevant specialisations, undergraduate courses, colleges, learning plans, projects and internship routes.

An assessment shaped around your stage and goals.

Class 10 focuses on exploration. Class 12 focuses on your current stream and intended direction, then asks follow-up questions that help compare relevant specialisations.

Your report combines your interests, responses to aptitude questions, chosen subjects and career direction. Use it to compare options and discuss important decisions with a qualified career counsellor.
